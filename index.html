Privacy Policy: Salvage Station

Last updated: 2026-11-25

Salvage Station ("the app") is a single-player game by Synapse One for Android and iPhone. This policy explains what data the app handles. Where the two versions differ, both are described.

Your game progress: your station, items, credits, level and settings are saved on your device. We never receive them (see Cloud save below for the optional backup). Uninstalling the app deletes them, and you can erase them at any time with Settings > Reset progress. On iPhone, the saved game is also part of your device backup (iCloud Backup or a computer backup), like other app data, if you have backups turned on.

Cloud save on Android (Google Play Games, optional): if you choose to sign in with Google Play Games (Settings > CLOUD SAVE, or the offer after stage 2), the app stores a copy of your game progress in your Google Play Games Saved Games, so you can restore it after reinstalling or on a new phone. This copy belongs to your Google account and is stored by Google; we run no server and never receive it. If you do not sign in, nothing is uploaded and your progress stays on your device only. Switching cloud save off in Settings stops further uploads; to delete the stored copy, use Google Play Games (Play Games app > Settings > Delete Play Games account and data). Resetting your progress in the app also replaces the cloud copy with the fresh game. Google's policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Cloud save on iPhone (iCloud, optional): if you turn on cloud save (Settings > CLOUD SAVE, or the offer after stage 2), the app keeps a copy of your game progress in iCloud key-value storage under your Apple Account, so you can restore it after reinstalling or on another iPhone. This copy is stored by Apple; we run no server and never receive it. If iCloud is off for the app, nothing leaves your device. Switching cloud save off in Settings stops further uploads; to delete the stored copy, use the iPhone Settings app (your name > iCloud > Manage Account Storage, then Salvage Station). Apple's policy: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

Achievements and leaderboards (optional): on Android they go through Google Play Games once you have signed in for cloud save. On iPhone they go through Apple's Game Center, and only after you open the achievements or leaderboards in the game for the first time; Game Center then receives which achievements you unlocked and your scores, linked to your Game Center profile. You can sign out of Game Center in the iPhone Settings app at any time.

Advertising (Google AdMob): the app offers optional rewarded ads (for example, watch an ad for free energy). No ad is ever shown unless you choose to watch one. To serve and measure ads, Google's Mobile Ads SDK collects and processes data such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location derived from it, device and app information (including crash and performance data), and ad interactions. Google may use this data for personalized advertising where you allow it. In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before any ad is requested (Google's consent form); you can change your choice at any time in Settings > AD PRIVACY CHOICES. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your Android settings. On iPhone, the first time you choose to watch an ad the app asks for permission to track (Apple's App Tracking Transparency); only if you allow it can Google use the iPhone's advertising identifier, and ads work either way. You can change this at any time in the iPhone Settings app (Privacy & Security > Tracking). Google's policy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Purchases (Google Play, App Store): in-app purchases are processed by Google Play on Android and by Apple's App Store on iPhone. We do not receive your payment details. The app only receives a confirmation from the store of which product was bought, so it can deliver it and restore one-time purchases on a new device.

Analytics and crash reports: from version 1.1, to see which parts of the game work and to fix crashes, the app sends anonymous gameplay events (for example "stage reached", "screen opened", how long a session lasted, or that a purchase was started or completed, without any payment details) and error reports (where in the game's code an error happened, without any file paths from your device) to our analytics provider, PostHog (servers in the EU). From version 1.1.9 they also include basic device information (phone model, Android or iOS version, graphics chip, processor type and core count, memory size, screen size), so we can fix problems that affect particular phones. They are tied only to a random number created when the app is installed, never to your name, account, advertising ID or device ID. You can switch this off at any time in Settings > PRIVACY > Share usage data; events that have not been sent yet are then deleted. Uninstalling the app deletes the random number. PostHog's policy: https://posthog.com/privacy

Data sharing: we do not sell your data. Data is shared only with Google as described above (AdMob for ads, and on Android Google Play for purchases and Google Play Games for the optional cloud save and achievements), with Apple on iPhone (the App Store for purchases, iCloud for the optional cloud save, Game Center for optional achievements and leaderboards) and with PostHog for anonymous analytics and crash reports, encrypted in transit. The app also downloads a small news and events file from our web page (GitHub Pages); that request carries no game data.

Children: the app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal data from them. Ads are limited to content rated for general audiences.

Changes: if the app starts handling data in a new way, this policy will be updated before that version is released.
